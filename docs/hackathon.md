## Success metrics

The prototype succeeds if:

- a new user understands the product in <10 seconds
- scan-to-result works end-to-end
- live external product data is visible
- prices are useful and credible
- at least one virtual try-on is genuinely generated
- the visual reveal feels impressive
- the entire demo fits comfortably inside 1–2 minutes
- the sponsor integrations are obvious without reading the source code

## Why RealityLens can win

### Concept

It connects the physical and digital shopping worlds.

Search normally begins with words.

RealityLens begins with **something the user can see**.

### Technical execution

The project combines two different capabilities into one coherent interaction:

```text
visual discovery
+
live commerce data
+
generative virtual try-on
```

The integrations are not decorative. Remove either core provider and the product loses a central part of its value.

### Feasibility

The prototype does not require RealityLens to invent:

- visual search infrastructure
- global product indexing
- retailer scraping
- virtual try-on models

The APIs supply these primitives.

RealityLens supplies the product experience connecting them.

### Startup direction

Potential future business models:

- affiliate revenue
- retailer integrations
- mobile shopping app
- white-label visual shopping SDK
- marketplace integration
- fashion discovery platform

These are future directions, not MVP requirements.

## Submission story

### Problem

People constantly see products they like in the real world but do not know:

- what the product is
- where it is sold
- whether it is available cheaper elsewhere
- how it would look on them

Traditional shopping starts with a query.

But often the user has no query.

They only have an image.

### Solution

RealityLens lets the image become the query.

Take a photo.

RealityLens searches the web visually, finds relevant commerce results, compares options, and turns discovery into a virtual fitting experience.

### Sponsor integration story

#### SerpApi

SerpApi is not a background utility.

It powers the moment where a real-world photo becomes live, structured web/product information.

#### Perfect Corp

Perfect Corp is not a decorative filter.

It powers the second half of the experience:

> turning discovery into personalized visualization.

## Demo script

### 0–5 seconds

Hold up / show a real product.

Say:

> “Shopping usually starts with knowing what to search for. But what if all I have is this?”

### 5–15 seconds

Open RealityLens and photograph the product.

> “RealityLens makes the camera the search bar.”

Show scanning animation.

### 15–30 seconds

Reveal product and merchant/pricing results.

> “It visually finds the product and turns live web results into something I can actually shop.”

### 30–50 seconds

Tap Try On.

Upload pre-approved suitable user photo.

> “Finding it is only half the problem. I still want to know how it looks on me.”

Generate/reveal try-on.

### 50–60 seconds

Before/after slider.

> “See it. Scan it. Try it. Buy it.”

End.
